Privacy Policy

Zerif Secim Social Manager — Last updated: September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Zerif Secim Social Manager may process information when users connect supported social media accounts and use social media management and publishing features.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on the connected platform and permissions granted by the user, the service may process information such as account identifiers, profile information, authorization information, access tokens, publishing permissions, and information required to provide requested social media features.

2. Content Information

The service may process text, images, videos, captions, scheduling information, and other content that users choose to prepare or publish through the service.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used only as necessary to provide requested functions, including connecting authorized social media accounts, preparing content, scheduling content, publishing content, and maintaining the operation of the service.

4. TikTok Integration

When a user connects a TikTok account, authentication and authorization are performed using TikTok's official authorization services. Zerif Secim Social Manager may request only the permissions required for the enabled TikTok features.

TikTok information is used only to provide functionality requested by the authorized user, including account connection and supported content publishing functions.

5. Facebook and Instagram Integration

Facebook and Instagram integrations may use official Meta authorization mechanisms and APIs. Information obtained through those integrations is used only for features requested by the authorized user.

6. Third-Party Services

Zerif Secim Social Manager may interact with third-party platforms such as TikTok, Facebook, and Instagram. Those platforms have their own privacy policies, terms, and data practices.

7. Data Sharing

Personal information obtained through connected social media accounts is not sold to advertisers. Information may be transmitted to the relevant social media platform when necessary to perform an action requested by the user, such as publishing content.

8. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, support authorized integrations, comply with applicable requirements, or maintain service functionality.

9. Revoking Access

Users may revoke permissions granted to connected social media platforms through the relevant platform's account settings where supported.

After authorization is revoked, features that require that authorization may stop working.

10. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information associated with the service. No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

11. Children's Privacy

Zerif Secim Social Manager is intended for users who are permitted to manage the social media accounts they connect to the service. The service is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable platform or legal requirements.

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the service, integrations, or applicable requirements change. The current version will be published on this page.

13. Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be submitted through the official Zerif Secim Social Manager project repository.
